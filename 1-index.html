Shmuel Alef 17, pesukim 4–7

גָּלְיָת שְׁמוֹ מִגַּת גָּבְהוֹ שֵׁשׁ אַמּוֹת וָזָרֶת

Golyas
6 amos and a zeres
6½ amos, about 10 ft 3 in
Shield bearer
3 amos
about 4 ft 9 in

Feet use Rav Chaim Na’eh’s amah of 18.9 in (48 cm). The shield bearer is shown at a regular man’s height, 3 amos, as Daas Sofrim writes on pasuk 4.

Also shown

Copper helmet (pasuk 5), spearhead of 600 iron shekels (pasuk 7), and his sword in its sheath (pesukim 45, 51).

The pesukim don’t give the size of the spear, sword or shield, so those are drawn to fit. The shield is drawn as woven wicker on a wooden frame, light enough for one man, resting on the ground and held by two grips and a shoulder strap.
